Strategy Notes

The concept balances rental yield, privacy, climate response, and practical serviceability for a medium-budget Sagar MP build.

Vastu-Aware Planning

East entry frontage, NE-friendly pooja and water concept, SE-leaning kitchen and stair, SW master placement, with compromise notes for the constrained three-side-blocked plot.

Heat And Ventilation

Balcony shading, light facade colors, OTS shafts, ventilated bathrooms, stairwell exhaust, terrace waterproofing slope, and provision for jaali or louvered front screens.

Water And Electricity

Underground sump in front/NE zone, pump to overhead tank, stacked plumbing, separate meter panel near common entry, and tenant-wise electrical separation.

Rental Income Mix

Ground floor as premium family 2BHK, first floor split into 1BHK plus single-room unit, and second floor studio for caretaker, staff, or low-cost rental.

Future Expansion

Plan assumes future one-floor readiness, but columns, footings, slab design, stair headroom, water pressure, and approvals must be checked before construction.

Validation Summary

Concept pass Program requirements are represented. Remaining risk is structural, municipal, drainage, setbacks, fire access, and site-specific dimensions.
