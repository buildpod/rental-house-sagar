# Project Brief: Rental Investment House, Sagar MP

## 1. Objective
To design a practical, medium-budget, Vastu-aware, rental-investment house on a 30 ft x 50 ft (1500 sq ft) East-facing plot in Tilli Colony, Sagar, Madhya Pradesh.

## 2. Core Constraints
- **Plot Dimensions:** 30ft width (East road front) x 50ft depth (West back).
- **Blocked Sides:** North, West, and South sides are completely blocked by existing structures.
- **Budget:** Medium (optimized for rental ROI).

## 3. Floor Requirements
- **Ground Floor:** 1x 2BHK unit for family living. Must include covered SUV parking, bike parking, 2 bathrooms, and a Pooja space.
- **First Floor:** 1x 1BHK unit + 1x Single Room unit (with attached bath). Must include a front East balcony.
- **Second Floor:** 1x Studio / Caretaker unit with large open terrace access.
- **Future Expansion:** Structure must be designed for an eventual G+3 (one additional floor).

## 4. Key Design Decisions
- **Staircase:** Located in the South-East (Front-Left) corner. This ensures upper-floor tenants can enter directly from the street without walking through the ground floor family's parking or living space, maximizing privacy.
- **Ventilation:** Two Open-To-Sky (OTS) shafts have been designed. A central shaft ventilates the living and common baths, while a rear-west shaft ventilates the rear bedrooms.
- **Water Management:** Underground sump in the North-East corner (under parking) with a submersible pump pushing to an overhead tank on the top terrace.
