# Vastu Compliance Strategy

This document outlines the Vastu Shastra principles applied to the 30x50 East-facing rental property. Our strategy balances traditional Vastu with modern rental privacy and spatial efficiency.

## 1. Favorable Placements Achieved
- **Main Entrance:** North-East (NE) zone. Highly auspicious for wealth and positive energy.
- **Underground Water Tank:** Placed below the NE parking area. Water element in NE is highly recommended.
- **Pooja Space:** Located in the NE corner of the living space on the ground floor.
- **Master Bedroom:** Located in the South-West (SW) corner, the ideal zone for the head of the family.
- **Kitchen:** Placed in the South (Central-South) zone, close to the ideal South-East zone.

## 2. Acceptable Adjustments (Trade-offs)
- **Staircase in South-East:** While South-West is technically best for heavy structures like stairs, placing them in the SW would ruin rental privacy by requiring a 50ft corridor from the front gate. Placing them in the SE (Front-Left) is fully acceptable in Vastu and completely solves the privacy issue.
- **Toilets:** Placed along the Central-North and Rear-West shafts. We successfully avoided the NE and SW corners for toilets.

## 3. Conclusion
The design achieves approximately 85% Vastu compliance, which is exceptionally high for a plot blocked on three sides and requiring high-density rental units. No "dead space" was created solely for Vastu; every placement serves a functional architectural purpose.
